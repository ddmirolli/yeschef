How does your kitchen ship?

Both modes show you every shared ticket, live. They differ in who says Go.

Open kitchen

Each dev ships under your repo's own rules. You watch the whole line and step in only when something needs you.

  • Collisions, unplanned work and changes to shared code come to The Pass
  • Changes to shared code still wait for a Go
  • Nothing else waits on you

Fits teams that merge many times a day.

French Brigade

Every ticket waits at Plating for a Go from you or a sous chef.

  • Everything in Open kitchen
  • Go, Refire or Hold on each ticket at Plating
  • Sous chefs can say Go if you let them

Fits teams where a reviewer signs off every merge.

You can change this any time in Settings, then Kitchen rules, for every project or just one.
